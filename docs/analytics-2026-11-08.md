## What this says (small sample; treat as a direction, not proof)
1. **Packaging decides everything right now.** The only video that took off has a story title (a country, a threat,
   a turn: "...Then It Filled"). Record-number titles ("Lost 22 Feet", "Broke Its 1977 Record Low", "Hit Record
   Lows") got 0-1.8 % CTR. Rule: lead with actors and conflict; keep the number for the thumbnail or the hook.
2. **Suggested traffic is where the impressions are (1,613) and its CTR is 1.3 %.** Next to big channels our
   thumbnail must read at 160 px: one map, one red subject, 2-3 words.
3. **Search viewers leave at 0:25.** The first 20 s must show exactly what the title promised, at once.
4. Views came in one burst (peak ~60/day on 3 Nov, GERD) and faded within 5 days: the feed tested the video and
   moved on. Uploading regularly gives the feed more tests.
