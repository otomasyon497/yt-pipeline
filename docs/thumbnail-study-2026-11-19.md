# Thumbnail study, 2026-11-19

48 long videos from the last 12 months in our niche ("why almost nobody lives", sinking cities / islands / airports,
land reclamation, seas drying up), ranked by views per channel subscriber (YouTube Data API, read-only). Sheets of the
thumbnails were reviewed by eye.

Top: "Japan Built a $20 Billion Airport on the Ocean - Now It's Sinking" 290x (2.95 M views, 10 K subs); "Dubai's $80M
Palm Villas Are Sinking" 173x; "Why Almost Nobody Lives in Uruguay" 116x; "They Moved the Ocean to Build It" 112x;
"Japan's $20 Billion Airport Is Slowly Sinking Into the Ocean" 105x; "The Moment the Atlantic Filled a Desert" 105x.

What the winners share:
1. One subject seen from a low oblique aerial angle (an island, an airport, a palm, a coast), filling most of the frame.
2. Saturated colour: turquoise water, bright land, a light sky at the top.
3. 0-3 huge words, white or yellow with a thick black outline ("SINKING", "$12 BILLION & SINKING", "95% OF URUGUAY
   IS EMPTY"); the biggest Kansai hit has no words at all.
4. Sometimes one red arrow or a cut-away of the ground.
5. Most are AI renders. Ours stay real: tools/cover_oblique.py tilts our own Sentinel-2 image into the same grammar.

Our flat painted-map covers (13 Nov on) and dark satellite covers share none of 1-2, and their CTR so far is lower
(docs/analytics-2026-11-19.md).
