## Florida Keys / Overseas Railroad and Highway
- First train reached Key West 22 Jan 1912 with Flagler aboard (Wikipedia, NPS itinerary, Keys Weekly).
- 1912 company booklet: Homestead to Key West 128.4 mi, 17.2 mi on bridges (11.1 mi concrete arch viaduct, 6.1 mi steel
  on concrete piers); Seven Mile Bridge was four bridges in series. CHECK primary.
- Cost: sources disagree (>$20 M vs ~$50 M): do not use until a primary figure is found.
- Labor Day hurricane 2 Sep 1935: 892 mb landfall (NOAA AOML blog), 40-41 mi of track lost (sources differ); line not rebuilt;
  state bought the right-of-way ($640,000 per one source, CHECK); Overseas Highway opened 1938 (FL State Parks).
- Bridges replaced in the 1980s; historic bridges NRHP 2004; Old Seven Mile Bridge reopened as a trail (Smithsonian).

## Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel (Virginia)
- Opened 15 Apr 1964 (Encyclopedia Virginia; ASCE); 17.6 mi shore to shore (CBBT site: ~20 mi with approaches).
- Northbound span $200 M from revenue bonds, no state or local tax money (CBBT; Encyclopedia Virginia).
- Four artificial islands (~5.25 acres each per CBBT; PBS says ~10: use CBBT), two tunnels ~1 mi each under the Thimble
  Shoal and Chesapeake channels; parallel span 1999 ($250 M), Parallel Thimble Shoal Tunnel project (2020s, CHECK).
