## Gaps we can own
- **Perth's water**: the one city is running out of rain. Dam inflows ~420 GL a year before 1975, ~75 GL in the last
  decade, ~12.5 GL in 2026; desalination now about half of Perth's water. Neither rival has it, and it is a chart.
- **Same latitude, two coasts** as the spine: Carnarvon (~230 mm) vs Brisbane (~1,030 mm) at about the same distance
  from the equator; the wind, not the latitude, decides.
- **Wittenoom**: a mining town that was shut and erased (degazetted 2007, last resident out 2022, >2,000 deaths).
- **Port Hedland**: 577.7 Mt a year leaves from a town of ~15,000; wealth that does not need a city.
- Batavia 1629 told as a story (125 murdered), not a date in a list.
