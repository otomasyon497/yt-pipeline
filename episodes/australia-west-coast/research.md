## Perth's water
- R5 Water Corporation: before 1975 Perth's dams averaged 420 billion litres of streamflow a year; the last decade
  ~75 GL; 15-20 % less rain since the mid-1970s gave ~80 % less streamflow; 2026 little more than 12.5 GL, the lowest
  in about a decade. Record low 2015 (15.8 GL in one release; 11.4 GL elsewhere: resolve in round 2).
  https://www.watercorporation.com.au/about-us/media-releases/2025/september-2025/wet-winter-belies-true-impact-of-declining-streamflow-to-perth-dams
  https://www.watercorporation.com.au/about-us/media-releases/2026/september/every-drop-counts-after-dry-winter-delivers-little-to-wa-dams
  https://www.watercorporation.com.au/our-water/rainfall-and-dams/streamflow (annual series for a chart: check)
- R6 Desalination: Kwinana and Binningup can meet about half of Perth's annual urban supply (BoM National Water
  Account 2025); ERA: 51.5 % of Perth's water from desalination in 2016-17; Alkimos plant +50 GL a year from 2028.

## History
- R7 Dirk Hartog left a pewter plate nailed to a post on 25 October 1616 on what is now Dirk Hartog Island, Shark
  Bay; oldest known European artefact in Australia (WA Museum; NMA). https://museum.wa.gov.au/explore/dirk-hartog/hartogs-plate
- R8 Batavia struck a reef in the Houtman Abrolhos on 4 June 1629; Pelsaert sailed to Batavia for help; mutineers
  led by Jeronimus Cornelisz killed 125 men, women and children (WA Museum database; other counts "at least 115");
  two mutineers marooned on the mainland. https://museum.wa.gov.au/maritime-archaeology-db/roaring-40s/batavia
- R9 Goldfields Water Supply Scheme: 566 km of steel pipe from Mundaring Weir to Kalgoorlie, eight steam pumping
  stations, opened 24 January 1903; C. Y. O'Connor died by suicide on 10 March 1902, before it was finished
  (WA Government heritage sheet; Engineers Australia; Wikipedia). Note: our interior episode showed Kalgoorlie, not
  the pipeline.

## Mining
- R11 Pilbara Ports 2024-25: iron ore exports 730.8 Mt; Port Hedland 577.7 Mt, Australia's largest bulk export
  port; Pilbara Ports ~81 % of national and ~43 % of global seaborne iron ore trade (WA Government media statement
  24 July 2025). https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Cook%20Labor%20Government/Pilbara-Ports-achieves-record-throughput-for-sixth-consecutive-year--20250724
- R12 Wittenoom: CSR closed the asbestos works in December 1966 (for economic reasons per the National Archives
  blog); phase-down from 1978; power cut Dec 2006; degazetted June 2007; last resident evicted Sept 2022; more than
  2,000 of ~20,000 former workers and residents have died of asbestos diseases (Asbestos Diseases Society / WA Gov);
  ~3 Mt of tailings in the gorge (WA Gov). https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/McGowan%20Labor%20Government/McGowan-Government-to-finalise-closure-of-Wittenoom-20190320
