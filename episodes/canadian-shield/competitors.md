## Gaps we can own

  and holds 5.64 million people. Why does Finland fill its shield and Canada not? Spine of the episode.
- **Hudson Bay, the freezer in the middle**: Churchill at 58.7 N (Stockholm's latitude) has polar bears; ice-covered
  bay cools the whole Shield. Climate normals as a chart (Churchill vs Helsinki/Stockholm).
- **The Clay Belt ended in fire**: Matheson 1916 (~223 dead, deadliest fire in Canadian history), Haileybury 1922.
