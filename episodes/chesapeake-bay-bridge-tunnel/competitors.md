## Our angle (not covered by any of the three)
The map story: **why the mouth of the bay is 17 miles of open water with two deep channels in it**.
- The bay is the drowned valley of the Susquehanna, flooded as the Ice Age ended (USGS).
- Under the bay mouth lies the Chesapeake Bay impact crater, ~35 million years old, ~85 km wide (USGS): the crater is
  why rivers bend toward here, and it still leaves salty groundwater and settling ground. CHECK all with USGS.
- Hampton Roads: fastest relative sea-level rise on the US East Coast (land subsidence + rising sea): Naval Station
  Norfolk floods. CHECK NOAA Sewells Point gauge, USGS subsidence.
