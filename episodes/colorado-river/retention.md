## Lessons for the next script
- Same pattern as gerd-nile: the hook is followed by policy detail and then a history / geography chapter, and
  almost everyone leaves there (43 % -> 14 % within a minute).
- Units the audience does not feel (acre-feet) in the first minute cost viewers; translate them or hold them back.
