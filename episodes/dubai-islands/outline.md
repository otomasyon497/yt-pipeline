# dubai-islands - outline (gate 1)

Working title: "Dubai Built Manhattan in the Sea. Then the Money Ran Out" (yt-package + thumbnail-design pass after
the script). Thumbnail idea: our own 2001 | 2026 Landsat split, 1-3 words ("FROM NOTHING?" / "58 KM2 OF SAND").
Target 2,400-2,700 words = about 17-19 min at narration 1.08. Every number from research.md (R1-R7).

Hook rule from the Studio data: the story starts at 0:00 and the first chapter turn comes with a new open question,
not background (gerd-nile lost half its viewers between 0:30 and 1:30).

1. Cold open (0:00-1:00, ~150 w): May 2001, empty turquoise sea (Landsat) -> wipe to 2026. "We measured it: about
   58 km2 of new land, roughly Manhattan." Promise: how you build a country's coastline out of the seabed, what it
   cost, and whether it is holding. Question card 1 (two-sided): the boldest engineering of the century, or a sand
   castle?
2. "Seventy Kilometres of Beach" (~300 w): Dubai's oil peaked in the early 1990s; a 72-km coast; tourism needs
   beachfront -> the palm shape multiplies the shoreline. ISS 2003 photo: the first palm half-built.
3. "How to Build an Island" (~450 w): 90+ million m3 of seabed sand sprayed into place, vibro-compaction, 7 million t
   of rock, the crescent; the stagnant-water mistake and the two cuts (13-14-day renewal). Landsat 2003 -> 2006.
4. "The World" (~400 w): 300 islands, 320 million m3 of sand, 37 million t of rock; last stone 10 Jan 2008; the islands
   sold... Question card 2: would you buy an empty island?
5. "The Crash" (~350 w): 25 Nov 2009, $26 bn standstill; Nakheel's $3.52 bn bond; Abu Dhabi's $10 bn on 14 Dec.
   Landsat sequence: Palm Jebel Ali frozen 2009-2022; Palm Deira shrinks into Deira Islands -> Dubai Islands.
6. "Is It Sinking?" (~350 w): the 2009 5-mm claim, Fugro's correction, Nakheel's 25 mm per century; no published
   verdict. Environment both ways: buried reefs and turbidity vs new rock habitat. Question card 3.
7. "The Comeback" (~400 w): Palm Jumeirah leads Dubai's $10m+ deals in 2026; Palm Jebel Ali relaunched 2023, villas
   sold out in hours, $950 m of contracts in April 2026; Sentinel-2 2022 vs 2026 shows it moving; The World still
   mostly empty; Dubai plans more coastline (Semafor 2026).
8. Close (~150 w): the new-land map one last time; closing question (pinned comment): would you live on land that was
   seabed 25 years ago? No generic CTA; one subscribe line tied to the measurement in chapter 1 or 7.

Visual inventory: satellite (own) ~8 views incl. 3 wipes; archive ~15 public-domain / CC stills (ISS 2003-2026,
ASTER 2008, two Commons ground photos); own graphics ~10 (stat cards, sand bar chart, maps, question cards).
No stock, no AI. Gap: no free construction film -> construction told with ISS 2003 + Landsat steps + map diagrams.

Open risks: oil-share and Palm Jumeirah sand / breakwater numbers need primary sources (else worded as ranges);
"sinking" stays two-sided; no contractor names.
