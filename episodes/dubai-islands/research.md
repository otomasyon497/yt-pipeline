# dubai-islands - research (in progress)

Status: first pass; every number needs a primary source before the script uses it.

## Palm Jumeirah
- Reclamation began June 2001; first residents end of 2006 (Nakheel, via Wikipedia "Palm Islands").
- Sand: sources range 92-120 million m3 (archived developer site: 92,234,000 m3); rock ~7 million t from 16 quarries,
  breakwater ~11 km. TO CHECK against Nakheel / engineering papers.
- "Sinking" (2009): Fugro NPA quoted at 5 mm/yr; Fugro said it was taken out of context (< 1 cm/yr, normal
  settlement); Nakheel: max 25 mm over 100 years. No verified peer-reviewed rate found yet. TO CHECK.

## The World
- ~300 islands, 4-6 km offshore, built 2003-2008. Heart of Europe (Kleindienst) on six islands; completion dates
  slipped (2026 -> end 2027, 52 % progress per Gulf News, date unconfirmed). Most islands still undeveloped (CNN).

## Palm Jebel Ali
- Stalled ~2009 (financial crisis); masterplan relaunched 2023; Dubai Holding took over Nakheel 2024; April 2026:
  AED 3.5 bn contracts for 544 villas (WAM / Dubai Media Office); infrastructure due Q4 2026; first units 2027.
