## Our room
- Nobody answers "why is it empty" as a chain of reasons with own measurements: 69 % of the land, 13 % of the people
  (8.8 % without Bend), the southeast (Harney + Lake + Malheur) 29 % of the land and 1.1 % of the people (M1).
- The failed homestead rush of 1905-1920 (Fort Rock valley: ~1,200 people by 1910, dozens of hamlets, one survives),
  the USDA buyback of the 1930s: not in any rival.
- The mill towns (Hines/Burns), the Malheur takeover 2016 tied to land ownership, the Harney Basin aquifer (critical
  groundwater area Dec 2025), Lower Umatilla nitrate wells, Google's water in The Dalles: live stakes.
- Rajneeshpuram told as a "why" chapter (a city of thousands built where nobody lived, and why it failed), not as true
  crime; the 1984 attack is our first sentence (hook guide rule 1).
