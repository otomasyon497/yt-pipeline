## Facts (round 1; [CHECK] = read primary in round 2)
- R1 Rajneeshee attack: Sept-Oct 1984, Salmonella Typhimurium on salad bars of ten restaurants in The Dalles (Wasco
  County), 751 sick, 45 hospitalised, none died; a trial run to sway the Nov 1984 county election; strain found in the
  commune's lab; largest bioterror attack in US history (Wikipedia; CDC via CHDS; Slate/Atlas Obscura). Some sources
  say eight restaurants -> say "ten restaurants" with CDC [CHECK Torok et al. JAMA 1997].
- R2 Rajneeshpuram: Big Muddy Ranch ~64,000 acres bought 1981 (~$5.75 M); a city incorporated; ~7,000 residents in
  1984, ~15,000 at the 1984 festival; Antelope (pop. under 100) taken over 1982 and renamed Rajneesh 18 Sept 1984;
  collapse 1985 (Oregon Encyclopedia; Wikipedia; Bend Bulletin) [CHECK numbers, deportation date Nov 1985].
- R3 Climate: Cascades rain shadow; Oregon record low -54 F at Seneca 10 Feb 1933 (also Ukiah); record high 119 F
  Pendleton 10 Aug 1898 (tied 2021 Pelton Dam / Warm Springs) (WRCC); Alvord Desert ~7 in a year
  [CHECK NOAA normals Portland vs Bend vs Burns].
- R4 Oregon Trail: 300,000-400,000 travelled 1840-1860 (Oregon Encyclopedia); route Powder River, Grande Ronde, Blue
  Mountains, Columbia to The Dalles, then rafts / Barlow Road (1846) to the Willamette; Whitman killings at Waiilatpu
  Nov 1847, Cayuse War (Britannica; Oregon Encyclopedia).
- R5 Homestead rush: Enlarged Homestead Act 1909 (320 acres); first Fort Rock valley claim 1905; ~1,200 people on
  dry-farm claims there by 1910; up to 1 in 6 claimants single women; drought and frost after ~1915/1918; 1930s USDA
  Fort Rock Valley Resettlement and Land Utilization Project bought abandoned claims; Fort Rock the only surviving town
  of several dozen hamlets (Oregon History Project "Dry-Farm Homesteading"; Oregon Encyclopedia "Fort Rock").
- R6 Timber: Hines mill (Edward Hines Lumber Co.): layoffs 1980, Harney County unemployment over 30 % by Feb 1981,
  equipment auctioned, reopened smaller; closed 2006; Burns + Hines peak ~8,000 -> 4,309 (2017) (Oregon Encyclopedia;
  Capital Press) [CHECK census for Burns/Hines].
- R7 Malheur National Wildlife Refuge occupation 2 Jan - 11 Feb 2016, 41 days; LaVoy Finicum killed 26 Jan 2016;
  Bundys and five others acquitted 27 Oct 2016 (NBC; SPLC) [CHECK federal share of Harney County land].
- R8 Harney Basin groundwater: declines >100 ft in one area, up to 8 ft a year; at least 70 wells dry; designated a
  critical groundwater area by the Water Resources Commission Dec 2025 (OPB 2025-12-12; OWRD/USGS).
- R9 Lower Umatilla Basin nitrate: ~4,500 domestic wells, ~12,000 people; Morrow County emergency 9 June 2022; 200+ of
  485 tested wells above the federal limit by Sept 2022 (Oregon Secretary of State audit; OPB).
- R10 Umatilla Chemical Depot: ~12 % of the US original chemical weapons stockpile stored from 1962; destroyed by
  incineration 2004 - 25 Oct 2011 (US Army; Oregon DEQ).
- R11 The Dalles: Google data center 2006; about a third of the city's water by 2024 (OPB 2026-01-15).
- R12 Bend: 2020 census 99,178 (city); Deschutes fastest-growing Oregon county 2010-2020 (+25.7 %, KTVZ)
  [CHECK 1990 census].
- R13 Greater Idaho: 13 eastern counties passed measures 2020-2024 (Baker, Crook, Grant, Harney, Jefferson, Klamath,
  Lake, Malheur, Morrow, Sherman, Union, Wallowa, Wheeler; Crook May 2024 the 13th); Idaho House non-binding memorial
  2023 [CHECK]; State of Jefferson 1941 (Fact Atlas; Ballotpedia; Wikipedia).
