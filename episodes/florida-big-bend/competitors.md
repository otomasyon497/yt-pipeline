## Table stakes (every competitor has these; we must too, sourced)
- No beaches / no barrier islands from the Ochlockonee to Anclote Key; sediment-starved, low wave energy, very gentle shelf.
- Karst: sinkholes, rivers that vanish (Aucilla), giant springs.
- Cedar Key: Yulee's cross-Florida railroad (1861), pencil cedar (Faber, Eagle), 1896 hurricane, Henry Plant takes the trade to Tampa (1884).
- Today: tiny towns (Cedar Key ~700, Horseshoe Beach), no interstate, Foley mill closure 2023.
- Idalia 2023 (Keaton Beach), Debby 2024 (Steinhatchee), Helene 2024 (near Perry): three landfalls in 13 months.
- Protected land: St Marks NWR, Big Bend Seagrasses Aquatic Preserve, "Nature Coast".
