## What they leave out (our room)
- **The pipe:** nobody tells that the islands have no usable fresh water of their own (porous reef rock), that the Navy
  built the pipeline from Florida City in 1941-42 (first water 22 Sep 1942), and that today one pipe along the road
  carries the whole chain's water. Only one passing mention (Geography Docs, one sentence).
- **One road = one door:** the 1982 Border Patrol roadblock at Florida City that cut the Keys off and led to the "Conch
  Republic"; the state's 24-hour evacuation rule, which is why building permits are capped (ROGO). Forgotten Road
  mentions the cap but not the reason.
- **Irma 2017** as a modern test of the one road (evacuation, the bridges reopened, 1,179 homes destroyed).
- **Own measurements:** sea-level rise at the Key West gauge since 1913 from NOAA's monthly data; share of the Keys'
  land under 3 / 6 ft (NOAA SLR DEM); how close the people live to US 1 (Census blocks).
- **The bill to keep it:** $1.8 B to raise 155 mi of county roads, Twin Lakes ~$22 M for under a mile, Seven Mile
  Bridge replacement ~$677 M (FDOT, ~2030).

## Our spine
One road carries everything, so the question is why it was built and whether it can be kept. Avoid: the full Key West
wrecking/cigar history (two rivals did it at length), a Flagler biography, the geology countdown, GTA.
