## Where viewers leave
1. 0:30 - 2:34 (63 % -> 30 %): right after the hook the video turns into a geography lesson (two halves of the
   Nile, Lake Tana, Khartoum) before the story's conflict comes back. Half of those still there at 0:30 go here.
2. 7:42 - 8:30 (23 % -> 15 %): a paragraph of engineering specifications in a row (145 m, 1.8 km, 5 km, 13
   turbines, 5,150 MW). A third of the remaining viewers leave during it.
3. After 8:30 the curve is flat: whoever passes the middle stays to the end.

## Lessons for the next script
- No background lecture between 0:30 and 2:30. Go from the hook straight into the conflict or the first measured
  surprise; bring geography in later, one fact at a time, when a scene needs it.
- No spec dumps: one dimension per point, and only with what it means ("taller than ...", "enough for ...").
  Three or more numbers in a row lose viewers.
- The middle (2:30 - 7:30) holds well when each chapter opens with a question; keep that.
