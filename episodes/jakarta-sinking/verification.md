  PMC13060580) — V. Sentinel-1 2017-2023 (ALOS-1 2007-2010/11 for comparison):
  - coastal exposure hot spots: "Jakarta (maximum subsidence: 3.6 cm/year; population: 100,000 people; average

    not the whole city);
  - "in Bandung, Pekalongan, and Demak, more than 30% of the city area is experiencing subsidence rates greater than

  - agricultural areas in Demak and Pati: mean rate up by 1-7 cm/yr vs 2007-2011; accelerations 0.1-0.2 cm/yr² in
    Pekalongan and Semarang;
