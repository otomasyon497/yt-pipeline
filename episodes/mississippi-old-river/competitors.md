## What they all share (so it is table stakes, not our edge)
- Hook: "the Mississippi is trying to leave; one structure stops it; in 1973 it almost won".
- 1953 Corps study: capture by ~1990 if nothing done; 70/30 split fixed by Congress (latitude of Red River Landing);
  Old River Control finished 1963; 1973 wing wall collapse, scour hole, 7 of 11 gates undermined, head limit 37 ft -> ~22 ft;
  auxiliary structure 1986, Sidney Murray hydro 1990; Morganza 1973 + 2011; corridor (port, refineries, drinking water, salt wedge).
- All narrated over stock / generic maps. None shows the delta lobes from space or measures anything.

## Our edge (maps + satellite + own measurements)
1. **The old deltas from space**: the six Holocene delta lobes (Maringouin, Teche, St Bernard, Lafourche, Plaquemines-Balize,
   Atchafalaya) as painted regions on a real map, in order: "the river has done this five times".
2. **The capture is already visible**: Wax Lake and Atchafalaya deltas are the only Louisiana coast that is growing:
   Landsat wipe 1984 -> 2025 at the Atchafalaya Bay (own area measurement). While the Birdfoot delta shrinks next door.
3. **How people made it worse**: Shreve's Cut 1831 (Turnbull's Bend, the oxbow still visible = "Old River"), the
   Atchafalaya raft cleared 1839-1860s: both on Sentinel-2 close views.
4. **The structure itself** close up (Sentinel-2: Old River Control, auxiliary, Murray hydro, Morganza), the 1973 story,
   2011 Morganza opening (MODIS/Landsat flood day vs normal).
5. **What if**: the channel below Baton Rouge is below sea level -> salt wedge (2023 saltwater wedge, underwater sill at
   Myrtle Grove) - a real recent event with satellite and official sources.
6. Trivia lists (Itasca, New Madrid, barges) are not our shape: one "why" with numbered reasons.
