## Facts so far
- Area 1,564,116 km2, 18th largest; landlocked between Russia and China; "most sparsely populated sovereign state";
  about 3.5 M people (2025 est.), 2.24 per km2; Ulaanbaatar has roughly half (Wikipedia Mongolia, A).
  O: France (metropolitan) 551,695 km2 -> Mongolia is 2.8 times as big.
- 2020 census 3,296,866 (Wikipedia infobox, A; table total 3,197,020 conflicts -> say "about three and a half million
  today"); density 1.76/km2 in 2020; Kazakhs 3.8 %.
- Climate: January averages as low as -30 C; over 250 sunny days; Ulaanbaatar annual mean -1.3 C, the world's coldest
  capital (Wikipedia Mongolia, A); average elevation about 1,580 m (Geography of Mongolia, A); Gobi rain 50-100 mm a
  year; the north about 310 mm (A); frost-free period at Ulaanbaatar mid-May to late August (A); arable land 0.7 %
  (CIA 2023 est., A); Gobi about 41.3 % of the territory (Springer article, A); 76.9 % of the land degraded (2020
  national desertification assessment, A).
- Livestock: 64.7 M alive at the end of 2023 (Save the Children citing official count, A); 2018: 66.46 M, a record
  since counts began in 1918; 230,800 herder households with livestock; sheep 46.0 %, goats 40.8 %, cattle 6.6 %,
  horses 5.9 %, camels 0.7 % (AKIpress, A).
- Dzud: 2009-10 more than 10.3 M animals, 25 % of the national herd (UN, A); 2022-23 4.4 M (A); 2023-24 about 7.2 M by
  24 April 2024 (FAO GIEWS via ReliefWeb, A), UNDP assessment 8.1 M (A).
- Herders after socialism: livestock privatized 1991-92; herder households about 70,000 in 1990 -> about 200,000 at the
  end of the 1990s (Yamamura chapter, A).
- Ulaanbaatar: planned for about 600,000, reached in 1990 (Diplomat photo essay, A); 1.6 M of 3.5 M (A); World Bank
  2018: more than half the residents, over 790,000 people, live in ger districts heated mainly with coal and wood;
  winter PM2.5 in ger areas more than 100 times the WHO 24-hour guideline (V-ish, World Bank feature, TODO read);
  80 % of winter air pollution from ger-district coal burning (A).
- Mining: about 78.8 % of exports in 2015 (USGS, A), around 80 % or more later (A); coal to China 29.8 M t in 2022, 94 %
  of coal exports (A); Oyu Tolgoi production from Jan 2013 (A); Tavan Tolgoi rail to the Chinese border from Sept 2022.
- History: Genghis Khan 1206; independence 1921; Mongolian People's Republic 1924, Soviet satellite; Choibalsan
  collectivized livestock; 1990 peaceful democratic revolution (Wikipedia Mongolia, A).
- Dinosaurs: Flaming Cliffs (Bayanzag), 1923, first recognized non-avian dinosaur eggs, Central Asiatic Expeditions of
  the AMNH under Roy Chapman Andrews; Oviraptor named for the nest (AMNH, A).
