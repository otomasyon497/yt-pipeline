# neom-the-line - outline (gate 1)

Working title: "Saudi Arabia Promised a 170 km City. We Measured What's Really There"
Angle: the promise against the satellite record, 2020 -> 2026, with our own measurements; the people who lived
there; why it stopped. Two-sided throughout. Word budget ~1,650 (about 11.5 min at our pace); the sourced material
does not carry 15 minutes without padding.
