## R1 Our own imagery and measurement (preliminary)
- Sentinel-2 L2A: 6 Jun 2020 vs 9 Aug 2026 over 35.45-35.75 E, 28.02-28.13 N: an empty wadi in 2020; a ruler-
  straight works corridor (roads, pads, plants, cut) in 2026. Works as the cold-open wipe.
- Full corridor 34.95-36.25 E, 27.95-28.20 N (2020-09/10 vs 2026-08-24), change image: a straight disturbed line
  from about 35.06 E / 28.12 N (near the Gulf of Aqaba coast) to about 35.93 E / 28.06 N (~86 km), and a separate
  eastern segment about 36.04-36.22 E (~18 km) with large pads. Rough total ~100 km of visible works, to be traced
  properly (tools/neom_numbers.py, TODO) before any number goes in the script.
- Compare: NEOM-shared aerial photos (2024) reportedly showed ~120 km excavated (secondary report, VERIFY); Soar
  Earth, Dec 2022: activity on about half the 170 km (MIT Technology Review, 9 Dec 2022).

## R2 The plan (2021-2022)
- Announced 10 Jan 2021 by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on state TV. Design shown July 2022: two mirrored
  walls, 170 km long, 200 m wide, 500 m high, 9 million residents (Guardian, 27 Jul 2022).
- Earthworks began October 2021 (Bloomberg). Wikipedia location 28.10 N, 35.30 E.
- Cost estimates: $100-200 bn (2021 reporting) up to $1 trn; first phase SAR 1.2 trn ($320 bn) per the crown
  prince (2022, via Bloomberg 2024).

## R3 The shrinking (2024-2026)
- Apr 2024: WSJ and Bloomberg: phase one 2.4 km by 2030; Saudi officials denied scaling back (CNBC).
- Oct 2024: AGBI: 5 km by 2030, full length rescheduled to 2045.
- Mar 2025: WSJ: about $50 bn spent; a 2024 internal board presentation put the original design at $8.8 trn and
  completion in 2080; NEOM said the report misinterpreted figures (VERIFY from WSJ text or a major outlet).
- 16 Sep 2025: PIF suspended construction pending a strategic review (Blick, 29 Sep 2025, via Wikipedia; VERIFY).
- 25 Jan 2026: 2029 Asian Winter Games at Trojena postponed indefinitely (The National; AGBI; MEED).
- 22 May 2026: Semafor exclusive: further work on The Line delayed until at least after 2030; 2030 resident target
  cut to up to 100,000; about $3 bn to continue OXAGON (port / industrial city); review led by CEO Aiman
  al-Mudaifer. Not officially cancelled.
- Jul 2026: AGBI: NEOM rewrites the pitch; The Line "a core component", "phased, demand-led" (page 403 from here,
  VERIFY wording).

## R4 People (two-sided, sourced only)

  April 2020 (Al Jazeera, 15 Apr 2020). Oct 2022: three Howeitat men sentenced to death for refusing to leave (BBC /

## R5 Criticism and defence
- Complexity Science Hub (npj Urban Sustainability, 2023): mean distance between residents 57 km vs 2.9 km in a
  circular city of the same area. Paul Oldfield (Dezeen 2022): ~1.8 Gt CO2e embodied; mirrored walls a barrier and
  bird hazard. Defence: NEOM's car-free, 20-minute end-to-end high-speed rail claim (VERIFY official wording).
