## What they leave out (our room)
- **The Big Burn, August 1910:** ~3 M acres in two days, ~85 dead, a third of Wallace burned, Ed Pulaski's crew in a
  mine tunnel.
- **The stolen capital:** Lewiston was the first capital (1863); the seal and archives were taken to Boise in 1865;
  the north asked to join Washington (GeoLex only mentions Washington letting the land go).
- **The poisoned valley:** Bunker Hill baghouse fire, Sept 1973, smelter kept running; children's blood lead among the
  highest ever measured in the US; Superfund 1983; plus the 1899 dynamited mill and the 1905 governor's murder.
- **The Sunshine Mine fire, 2 May 1972, 91 dead** (correct date) and the 1977 Mine Act.
- **One road north to south:** White Bird grade (US 95) the only link for ~40 years; US 12 over Lolo Pass only 1962.
- **62 % federal land** and the largest wilderness in the lower 48 (2.37 M acres).
- **Own measurements:** share of Idahoans living north of the Salmon River vs its share of the land; the empty belt.
- **The twist today:** Kootenai County +9.9 % since 2020; Navy submarines tested in a mountain lake.

Avoid: the vlog format, Geoff's whole-state listicle, the Missoula floods at length.
