## Cape Hatteras Lighthouse
- Completed 1870 about 1,500 ft from the ocean; by 1980 about 50 ft? (NPS page says ~50 ft by 1980; others 120 ft in 1999 —
  TODO V on NPS move FAQ https://www.nps.gov/caha/learn/historyculture/themovefaqs.htm).
- NPS move FAQ (V, read): moved 2,900 ft, 17 Jun - 9 Jul 1999, 23 days of moving after over a year of preparation,
  cost $11.8 million, now ~1,500 ft from the shoreline. Relit 13 Nov 1999 (NPS per search; TODO V).
  NPS "Moving the Cape Hatteras Lighthouse" (V, read): completed 1870 1,500 ft from the ocean; first (1803) lighthouse
  about one mile from the shore, 600 ft south, lost to a 1980 storm; 1970: only 120 ft from the ocean's edge (so "120 ft"
  is 1970, NOT 1999); before the move the ocean reached the base. 1936 Coast Guard groins, then light moved to a steel
  tower in Buxton Woods; 1960s-70s nourishment + three groins; 1980 seawall considered; 1988 National Academy of Sciences
  report recommended relocation; Jan 1997 NC State review endorsed it; FY1998 funds. Weight 4,830 tons; moved 2,900 ft
  southwest, 5 ft at a time, from 17 Jun 1999; on the new foundation 9 Jul 1999; back in service 13 Nov 1999; now 1,600 ft
  from the ocean (this page) vs ~1,500 ft (FAQ) -> say "about 1,500 feet". Cost $11.8 million (FAQ).
- Debate (two sides): relocation was rejected at first as impractical and debated for a decade (1988-1997).

## Science, framing
- USGS coastal-change page "Overwash" (archived, V): storm overwash carries sand over the dune to the landward side;
  repeated transfers move the island landward = "barrier island rollover"; overwash fans can span the island; Hurricane
  Isabel (2003) buried parts of Highway 12 under as much as a metre of sand.
  https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20081108033527/http:/coastal.er.usgs.gov/hurricanes/coastal-change/overwash.html
- USGS-led 2023 study (JGR Earth Surface, Dorian 2019, North Core Banks): sound-side flooding cut washout channels, island
  lost ~18 % of its above-water volume — complicates the classic picture. https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70242151 (A, optional)
- Point for the script: a fixed road and fixed houses stop the island's natural rollover; dunes built to protect NC 12 keep
  overwash off the island (TODO a source for the dune-line history, e.g. 1930s CCC dunes, NPS).
