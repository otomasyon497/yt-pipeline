## Our angle (draft)
Start from the map paradox: Russia's Pacific coast lies at the same latitude as the French Riviera, Vladivostok
at ~43 N like Nice, yet it has Siberian winters and almost nobody lives on it. Why? The Siberian High and the winter
monsoon blowing off the coldest land on Earth, the Sea of Okhotsk freezing down to Hokkaido, cold currents, fog,
permafrost reaching unusually far south. Then the human answers (treaties, rail, BAM, camps, money, 1991) shorter
than the rival's, with sourced numbers on screen, and the present: night lights, China across the Amur, the hectare.
