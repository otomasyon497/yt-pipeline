## 2. The climate paradox (our hook)
- A: Vladivostok 43.1 N; January daily mean -11.9 C, July 18.1 C (1991-2020 normals; Pogoda i Klimat / NOAA).
  Nice 43.7 N; January daily mean 9.5 C (1991-2020, Meteo-France). Gap ~21 C at the same latitude (OWN arithmetic).
  CHECK NOAA normals for both stations.
- A: Siberian High: September-April, centred near Lake Baikal, air near the centre often below -40 C; drives the East
  Asian winter monsoon (Britannica; Chang "The East Asian Monsoon"). Vladivostok's cold winters "owing to the influence
  of the Siberian High". CHECK a primary climatology source (NOAA/WMO/Britannica).
- V (via Wikipedia, NASA Earth Observatory 2023): the Sea of Okhotsk "is the southernmost sea in the Northern
  Hemisphere that freezes over seasonally"; 50-70 % ice-covered in a typical winter, Dec to early summer; February air
  ~-20 C in the northeast. CHECK NASA EO page text.
- V (WMO 14 Dec 2021): Verkhoyansk 38 C on 20 June 2020, Arctic record; station since 1885, ~115 km north of the
  Arctic Circle. A: Verkhoyansk -67.8 C (15 Jan 1885) as N-hemisphere low outside Greenland.

## 3. History
- A: Treaty of Aigun 28 May 1858: Russia received over 600,000 km2 between the Stanovoy Range and the Amur; Convention
  of Peking 14 Nov 1860: Ussuri krai / Primorye (~400,000 km2). Vladivostok founded 1860. CHECK Britannica.
- A: BAM 4,324 km, Tayshet - Sovetskaya Gavan; proposed March 1974, golden spike Sept 1984, declared complete 1991;
  ~US$14 bn; ~50,000 young volunteers; strategic alternative to the Trans-Siberian near the Chinese border. CHECK.
- A: Dalstroy formed 13 Nov 1931 (dissolved 1957) for gold and road building; prisoners at its facilities: 138,170

  death estimates vary widely (130,000 to >1,000,000; 10,251 recorded deaths in 1938): say "tens of thousands at least,
  estimates differ" or avoid a number.

# Gate-1 outline (draft, ~2,850 words)
Title: "Why Russia's Pacific Coast Is Almost Empty" (alt "Why Almost Nobody Lives in Russia's Far East").
1. Cold open: Vladivostok and Nice, same latitude: one has palm trees in January, the other a frozen bay people
   fish on. And north of it, an area the size of ~40 % of Russia with fewer than 8 M people.
2. The wind off the coldest land: Siberian High, winter monsoon, Oymyakon/Verkhoyansk, the 38 C swing.
3. A sea that freezes at the Riviera's latitude: Okhotsk ice, cold currents, short summers; the coast's few towns.
4. Ground that never thaws: permafrost, Yakutsk on piles.
5. Taken, not settled: Aigun and Peking treaties, Vladivostok, the Trans-Siberian ribbon (night lights).
6. Filled by force and money: Dalstroy, Kolyma, BAM, Soviet northern pay.
7. Emptied by 1991: Magadan -65 %, Chukotka -70 % (Rosstat), the hectare.
8. The edge of the Ring of Fire and the border: Kamchatka 2025, China's NE across the Amur. Closing question.
