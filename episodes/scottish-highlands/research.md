# Gate-1 outline (draft, ~2,800 words, ~17.6 min)
Title: "Why Almost Nobody Lives in the Scottish Highlands" (48 chars). Cover: bright glen photo + "EMPTIER THAN NORWAY" (cover_photo).
0. Cold open: Highland council area bigger than Wales, ~235,000 people, 9 per km2. Across the North Sea, Vestland:
   same ancient rock, same Atlantic rain, twice the density and a city of 290,000. Question: why is one full and one empty?
1. Rock and ice: Highland Boundary Fault, glaciers, thin soil, peat; the Flow Country (and the 1980s conifer rush).
2. The rain wall: western rainfall; but Norway gets the same -> nature is not the whole answer.
3. Clans to cash: Culloden 1746, chiefs become landlords.
4. The Clearances: sheep, Strathnaver 1814, Sellar acquitted 1816, crofts on the coast; ruined townships on satellite.
5. Hunger and ships: 1846 blight, emigration to Canada and Australia; 17 % -> 7 % of Scotland's people.
6. Deer forests and St Kilda: 1.98 M -> 2.57 M acres for sport (1883-1891); St Kilda 1930.
7. Norway's different path: freeholders and odal right, fishing farmers; power from the glens (Hydro Board 1943) lit
   the Highlands but did not refill them.
8. Who owns it now: ~400 owners hold half the private rural land (Wightman); Assynt, Eigg, Land Reform 2003; holiday homes.
9. Ending question: "Should Scotland break up the big estates to bring people back to the glens?"
