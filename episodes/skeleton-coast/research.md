  Namibia asked 2017; return agreed [CHECK date of return].
- R3 Walvis Bay: claimed by Britain 1878, annexed to the Cape 1884; Union of South Africa 1910; joint administration
  1992; Treaty on Walvis Bay signed 28 Feb 1994, in force 1 Mar 1994 (Britannica; Wikipedia; SAHO).
- R4 Swakopmund founded 1892 as the German port; the Mole opened Feb 1903, silted up within ~2 years; wooden jetty
  1905 (Wikipedia; Gondwana Collection) [CHECK].
- R5 Dunedin Star: aground at Clan Alpine Shoal, 29 Nov 1942; 21 passengers + 85 crew [CHECK: sources differ];
  tug Sir Charles Elliott wrecked; SAAF Ventura bogged and lost; two seamen died; survivors to Windhoek 24 Dec
  (Wikipedia; Marsh, Skeleton Coast; ASN).
- R6 Diamonds: Zacharias Lewala, April 1908 near Luderitz; Sperrgebiet proclaimed 1908 (~26,000 km2); Kolmanskop;
  Namdeb (Namibia-De Beers 50/50); Oranjemund company town, opened to the public 21 Oct 2017; Tsau //Khaeb NP 2008/2009
  [CHECK]; Bom Jesus (1533) found 2008 at Oranjemund (2,000+ coins, ~17 t copper).
- R7 Census 2023: Namibia 3,022,401 [CHECK]; Erongo 240,206 (+59 % since 2011, 150,809); Walvis Bay 102,704;
  Swakopmund 75,921 (NSA via New Era / Namibian).
- R8 Water and uranium: Erongo desalination plant (Areva/Orano, Wlotzkasbaken), inaugurated Apr 2010, first large

## Gate-1 outline (~2,750 words, ~17.3 min)
- Hook: ~1,500 km of Atlantic coast, a few towns; Walvis Bay + Swakopmund hold most coastal people; "But"; teasers:
  a harbour that belonged to another country until 1994; a ship rescue that wrecked a tug and a bomber; a town closed
  to outsiders until 2017. Question: why almost nobody lives on this coast?
- 1. The sea that will not rain (short, own rainfall numbers, fog).
- 2. No harbour, no water: Cao's cross; the one deep bay went to Britain; the Mole that filled with sand.
- 3. The coast of wrecks: Dunedin Star 1942 (re-hook story).
- 4. Diamonds closed the door: 1908, Sperrgebiet, Kolmanskop, Oranjemund until 2017, Bom Jesus.
- 5. Where people do live: Walvis Bay/Swakopmund boom, uranium + desalination, M1.
- 6. The next bet: green hydrogen in the park; ending question (open the coast, or keep it closed?).
