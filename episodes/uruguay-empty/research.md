## Own measurements (planned)
- **M1 Where Uruguayans live:** Kontur Population 2023 grid (HDX, as in Canadian Shield) -> share of people within 50 km of central Montevideo; share on the coastal departments; smallest area holding half the people; density of the interior (people per km2 outside the coast and the river towns).
- **M2 Same grass, different country:** Rio Grande do Sul (census 2022, IBGE) split into the forested north (old colonies) and the southern Campanha pampa: density of each vs Uruguay's interior. Hypothesis: Campanha is as empty as Uruguay; the people are on the old colony land.
- **M3 Cows per person by department:** DICOSE head by department / census 2023 by department.
