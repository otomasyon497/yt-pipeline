## What nobody shows (our edge)
- **Maps and satellite evidence**: none of them shows the lagoon from orbit. We can: the three inlets, the Canale dei
  Petroli cut across the lagoon (1960s), salt-marsh loss over time (Landsat wipes), Marghera next to the city.
